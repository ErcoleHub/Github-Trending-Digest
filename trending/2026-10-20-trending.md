## Editor's Take

Two repositories stand out for anyone creating content about AI agents this week. **feder-cr/git-ai** is the more provocative: it's a ground-up reimagining of git designed specifically for AI agents, signaling that the developer-tooling layer is being rebuilt from scratch to fit agentic workflows rather than adapted from human ones — that's a story arc worth tracking. **Leonxlnx/earshot** is the more immediately demonstrable: it gives coding agents an audio sense, letting them hear, review, and produce sound using only CPU, with MCP integration so tools like Claude Code can use it out of the box. The combination of "agents that code" plus "agents that can now hear and produce audio" is a concrete new capability with obvious content angles, and the CPU-only constraint keeps it accessible to anyone without GPU hardware.
