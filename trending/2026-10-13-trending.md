## Editor's Take

Two projects stand out for anyone covering AI agents and content creation. **`wheresryan22/anatomy`** is unusually specific: it's an agent skill — meaning it's designed to be called by an AI agent, not a human — that generates detailed isometric SVG illustrations of machines and systems, with optional WebGL shaders for 3D depth. The combination of agent-native design and visual output is rare and practically ready-made content for explaining how agent tooling is evolving beyond text. In the 30-day window, **`browser-use/jev-ultrafast`** from the browser-use org has pulled over 22,000 stars for what it calls the "fastest and cheapest web agent" — a direct shot at the cost and latency complaints that have slowed web agent adoption in production, making it highly timely if you're covering where agentic workflows are actually landing in real products. The `storytold` suite (wordcraft, photocraft, gridcraft, cadcraft, deckcraft, soundcraft) is dominating the trending charts this week with open-source Rust rewrites of the entire productivity and creative software stack, which is its own story, though it's orthogonal to the AI agent angle.
