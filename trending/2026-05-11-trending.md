## Editor's Take

The two repos most worth attention this week for anyone building or covering AI agents are **nexu-io/open-design** and **strukto-ai/mirage**. `open-design` is the more striking of the two: 37k stars in under 30 days signals real demand for a local-first, model-agnostic design environment that can output production-ready slides, prototypes, and videos directly from agent workflows — it fills the gap that Claude Design left for users who need portability and BYOK. `mirage` attacks a subtler but equally important problem: AI agents today are brittle because every environment (Docker, remote host, sandbox) exposes a different filesystem surface; by providing a unified virtual filesystem abstraction with FUSE and agent-native tooling, it makes multi-environment agent pipelines dramatically more composable. Both projects are early but pointed directly at the infrastructure layer that determines how capable AI agents become in practice.
