## Editor's Take

The two repos most worth attention for anyone creating content about AI agents are `langchain-ai/openwiki` and `mereyabdenbekuly-ctrl/clodex-ide`. OpenWiki, from the LangChain team, is a CLI that deploys AI agents to autonomously write and maintain your codebase's documentation — it's a direct, working answer to "can agents do knowledge work?" and its 11k stars in under a month signal real demand, not just hype. Clodex IDE is the week's most interesting newcomer: a local-first, zero-trust agentic IDE built around verifiable autonomous coding, with topics explicitly tagged `agentic-ai` and `ai-agents` — the "zero-trust" and "verifiable" framing reflects the current moment's growing appetite for AI agent workflows that don't require blind faith in the model. On the media side, `Robbyant/lingbot-video` is worth a closer look: a Mixture-of-Experts video pretraining project aimed at embodied intelligence, with topics spanning image-to-video and world-models, sitting at the intersection of AI agent perception and generative media in a way that few video repos do.
