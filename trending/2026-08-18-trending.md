## Editor's Take

This week was an unambiguous DeepSeek Harness moment — the framework launched on August 13 and hit 158k stars in five days, a velocity that rivals the earliest LangChain surge and signals something genuinely new rather than just another wrapper. DSH is built on the Cordis IoC container, which means every capability — models, tools, memory, even the UI — is a swappable plugin; for someone building AI-powered content pipelines, that modularity matters because you can compose research, drafting, and publishing agents without writing glue code, and swap any component independently. The secondary standout is Andrew Ng's `openworker`, which brings a more principled, curriculum-grounded approach to AI worker patterns — worth watching because Ng's projects tend to define the vocabulary the rest of the ecosystem adopts. Taken together, plugin-native agent runtimes appear to be the dominant architectural bet right now, and DSH's explosive adoption suggests the ecosystem may be converging on this pattern faster than anyone expected.
