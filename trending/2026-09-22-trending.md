## Editor's Take

This week is defined by a single story: the emergence of "Jev" — a family of small, fast, typed decision models that replace open-ended LLM generation with constrained boolean/categorical outputs. `browser-use/jev-ultrafast` hit 17K stars in under a week by applying this to web agents, cutting cost and latency dramatically compared to prompting a frontier model to "decide" in prose; `NandhaKishorM/laya` (15K stars in four days) appears to be the foundational typed-decision framework the whole ecosystem is building on, with satellites like `laya-mlx` (native Apple Silicon runtime) and `fast-jev-compaction` (context management for Claude Code) already orbiting it. For anyone creating content about AI agents, this is the infrastructure story right now: typed decision models as a lean, reliable steering layer sitting beneath agents — faster and cheaper than chain-of-thought, and more predictable than asking a big LLM to reason its way to a yes/no. `sapientinc/PRAXIST` is also worth a mention in the 30-day window as a serious autonomous research agent framing — measurable, executable, reproducible research — which is a distinct and credible take on what agents are for.
