## Editor's Take

The week's most important signal comes from **yc-software/qm**, a multiplayer agent harness from Y Combinator that accumulated nearly 11,000 stars in under a week — it's a coordination layer designed for multiple AI agents collaborating on real business tasks, and the YC origin makes it worth watching closely as a template for what the next generation of agentic startups actually looks like under the hood. Right alongside it, **andrewyng/openworker** — which landed in mid-July with no public description and has since crossed 12,600 stars — is almost certainly a foundational framework for deploying AI workers at scale; Ng doesn't ship quietly, and the name alone signals where the "AI workforce" discourse is heading. For content creators in the AI agents space, both repos are immediate fodder: qm gives you a concrete, demo-able multi-agent product with a YC pedigree, while openworker represents the open-source counterweight to proprietary agent platforms and will likely shape the vocabulary of the field over the next quarter. **microsoft/skill-recorder** is worth a sidebar mention too — the idea that AI can observe human work sessions and reconstruct them as reusable agent skills (rather than requiring explicit programming) is a genuinely new interaction model, and Microsoft shipping it under the Copilot umbrella means it will reach a lot of enterprise users fast.
