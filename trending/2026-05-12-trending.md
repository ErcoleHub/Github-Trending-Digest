## Editor's Take

The two repositories most worth attention this week for anyone creating content about AI agents are **nexu-io/open-design** and **strukto-ai/mirage**. Open-design accumulated 38k stars in under 30 days — an extraordinary signal — and it matters because it directly unbundles the design-generation capability that Anthropic has been centralizing in Claude Design, giving content creators and builders a self-hostable, BYOK alternative with genuine breadth (71 design systems, 19 agent skills). Mirage is quieter at 2k stars but solves a hard infrastructure problem: it gives AI agents a unified virtual filesystem abstraction so they can operate consistently across sandboxed, containerized, and local environments without the agent needing to reason about filesystem differences — exactly the kind of low-level plumbing that makes or breaks production agent deployments. If you cover the "agents actually doing things in the real world" angle, Mirage is the most technically substantive new project of the past week.
