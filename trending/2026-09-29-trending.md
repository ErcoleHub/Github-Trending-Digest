## Editor's Take

The standout this week for anyone covering AI agents is **KKKKhazix/AIHOT**, which gained nearly 2,800 stars in under two days. It's a self-hosted TypeScript framework that uses LLMs and MCP to automatically scan RSS feeds, identify trending topics, and write formatted daily reports — essentially a turnkey agentic content pipeline that anyone can point at their own domain. The fact that it's deployable with custom sources makes it directly actionable, not just a research demo. Equally worth attention is **browser-use/jev-ultrafast** from the 30-day window: the browser-use team has shipped what they're calling the fastest and cheapest web agent, building on the Jev decision-model ecosystem that's clearly having a moment — with laya (28k stars), kev, and jevgrep all cresting simultaneously, signaling that lightweight, fast inference models designed for agentic routing decisions are crystallizing into their own category right now.
