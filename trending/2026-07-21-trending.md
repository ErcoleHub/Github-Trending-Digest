## Editor's Take

Two repositories from this week's 7-day window stand out for anyone covering AI agents and content creation pipelines. **hoainho/img2threejs** is the most technically compelling: it routes a reference image through AI coding agents (the repo explicitly lists `claude-code` and `ai-agents` as topics) to produce a procedural, animation-ready Three.js 3D model entirely in code — no mesh files, no exports — and 1,300 stars in under a week signals genuine practitioner interest, not just viral novelty. Also worth a close look is **xiejunjie524/handdraw-story-video**, which converts rough hand-drawn story sketches into 35–45 second animated line-reveal videos; it's exactly the kind of low-friction creative pipeline tool that resonates with solo content makers who don't have 3D or animation skills. On the 30-day chart, **xai-org/grok-build** is the macro story: xAI has shipped a fullscreen TUI coding-agent harness in Rust and pulled 21k stars, a direct signal that the race to own agentic developer tooling is now multi-player and accelerating fast.
